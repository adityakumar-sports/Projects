# 📊 Sports Research

## 📌 Overview

This project represents my academic interest and practical work in **Sports Science, Sports Psychology, Research Methodology, and Performance Analysis**.

My research interests focus on understanding psychological and performance-related factors that can influence athletes' performance in competitive sports.

## 🎯 Research Focus

One of my key research interests is the comparative study of **mental toughness among badminton and table tennis players** competing at different levels.

The research explores psychological characteristics that may contribute to performance in competitive environments.

## 📊 Research Instrument

A key area of interest is the use of the **Sports Mental Toughness Questionnaire (SMTQ-14)** for assessing dimensions of mental toughness in athletes.

The questionnaire provides a structured approach to examining psychological characteristics relevant to competitive sports.
