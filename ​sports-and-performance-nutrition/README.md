# 🥗 Sports & Performance Nutrition

## 📌 Overview

This project focuses on the role of **sports nutrition in supporting athletic performance, recovery, training adaptation, and overall athlete wellbeing**.

It explores how appropriate nutrition and hydration strategies can help athletes meet the physical demands of training and competition.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Understand the nutritional requirements of athletes
* Support sports performance through appropriate nutrition
* Understand the importance of hydration
* Promote effective recovery after training and competition
* Explore nutrition strategies for different sporting demands
* Develop practical knowledge of athlete nutrition

## 🍎 Key Areas

The project covers:

* Macronutrients
* Micronutrients
* Hydration
* Pre-training & pre-competition nutrition
* During-training nutrition
* Post-training recovery nutrition
* Energy requirements
* Nutrient timing
* Recovery strategies

## 🧠 Knowledge & Skills Demonstrated

* Sports & Performance Nutrition
* Athlete Nutrition Planning
* Recovery Nutrition
* Hydration Strategies
* Fitness & Performance
* Application of Sports Science Principles
